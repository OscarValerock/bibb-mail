### Day +2 — Follow-up insight

CHF 240K per year. Invisible on the dashboard.

That is the number from the post this week. A client's outsourced headcount ratio was green every quarter. What was not measured: contractor rotation at 68% per year, each replacement triggering a full onboarding cycle. The data existed. The question had never been asked.

The academic name for this is formula incompleteness. The metric was built to answer one question. Over time that question became the only question anyone asked. The goal had other dimensions. The formula did not.

It applies everywhere — finance KPIs, HR dashboards, ops metrics. The pattern is the same. The measure looks fine. The business does not.

Four questions to run before any KPI goes live are in the post. Link in comments.
